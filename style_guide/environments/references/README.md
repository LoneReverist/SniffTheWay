## Night sequence direction

Focus on the dog and baby, their expressions and relationship, and the protective tree. Keep incoming and forward paths subtle, partly hidden by foliage, low contrast and secondary in every night page. Do not force both directions into the frame or widen/brighten the trail to explain the map. A small bend is acceptable; no intersection-like clearing. The moon stays above the forward bearing when that sky is visible. Camera changes must preserve one hollow and the same roots; the map defines offscreen connectivity, not mandatory onscreen path exposure.

Retain simplified blue/teal leaves, restrained light and broad quiet moss. Ground leaves can use the previously selected night candidate 3 treatment without importing its brighter lighting or moss detail.
