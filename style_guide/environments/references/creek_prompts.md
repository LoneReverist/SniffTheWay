# Accepted creek reference prompts

Built-in image_gen. Historical input names below describe the generation process; use the adjacent accepted PNGs for future work.

## Approach prompt

Edit the painted creek reference (image 1) to match revised layout guide (image 2). Main change: BOTH large landmark trees are on the WEST side of crossing, hence BOTH on SCREEN-LEFT in this north-facing approach view: original leaning entry-bank tree in left foreground remains, move the far-bank straight tree from right to LEFT of far path, clearly visible separately beyond creek beside foreground tree. Remove its old far-right trunk and roots, replacing with low shrubs/open forest space. Move far-bank pink/purple flower patch to RIGHT of far path, replacing its old left patch with quiet green shrubs. Keep broad gray foreground-right boulder. Keep original camera, three stepping stones and their sizes/spacing exactly: nearest oval S1, middle wide S2 notched on screen-right, far S3 flat far edge. Retain trail on both banks, teal creek, left-to-right flow and soft west light from left. Simplified dimensional storybook painting with broad leaf groups, restrained bark and sparse water highlights. No characters, labels, arrows, extra crossing stones or borders. Full-frame 16:9. Revised tree placements must be unmistakable: two large trunks on LEFT, no matching large trunk on RIGHT.

## Reverse prompt

Generate reverse camera view of revised creek reference image 1 using geometry guide image 2. Same location, camera now on north Bank B facing SOUTH across creek. BOTH landmark trees lie WEST of path, therefore BOTH appear SCREEN-RIGHT: straight T2 trunk close foreground RIGHT, leaning T1 tree far-bank RIGHT, clearly separate trunks across water. NO large landmark trunk on screen-left. Bank B pink/purple flowers now foreground LEFT, Bank A broad gray boulder now far-bank LEFT. Retain tree forms and material identity from reference, do not mirror it. Exactly THREE crossing stones: nearest S3 flattened north edge facing camera, middle widest S2 with east notch now on SCREEN-LEFT, far oval S1. Short same gaps and broad low warm-gray/tan slab tops, darker wet sides. Entry path beyond far bank recedes into woodland; foreground B path leads down to S3. Sun from world west now screen-RIGHT; water moves screen-RIGHT to LEFT with restrained broad wakes. Match simplified dimensional storybook painting, palette and time of day of image 1, quiet greens and teal water. No characters, arrows, labels, panels or border. Full-frame 16:9. Strongly asymmetric tree grouping on RIGHT balanced by low flowers and boulder on LEFT. Keep every stone present and avoid extra midstream stepping stones.
