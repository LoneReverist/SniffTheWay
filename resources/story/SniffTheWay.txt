Scene - Picnic
Page 1: Picnic
It was the perfect day for a picnic.
A curious baby.
A watchful dog.
And a family full of sunshine.

Page 2: Gust of wind
Then,
WHOOSH!

Page 3: Following butterflies
While the grown-ups chased the flying blanket,
small hands followed fluttering wings.
And protective paws stayed close behind.

Page 4: Lost
When the little one stopped,
his parents were nowhere to be seen.
He felt small and scared,
but a loyal nose was there to guide him home.


Scene - Creek
Page 1: Approaching Creek
By evening, the trees pressed in.
The path grew thin.
And soon, it disappeared into a creek.

Page 2: Baby reluctant to cross
To the little one, the water sounded enormous.
Too cold.
Too fast.
Too wide to cross.

Page 3: Crossing creek
But the dog pressed on,
and the little one bravely followed.
One careful step...
then another.

Page 4: Beyond the creek
On the other bank,
the boy smiled.
And together,
they continued on.


Scene - Night
Page 1: Dusk
The sun slipped behind the hills.
And the woods turned blue and quiet.

Page 2: Exhausted
The little one rubbed his tired eyes.
His brave heart had carried him far.
But even brave hearts need rest.

Page 3: Night
They curled up beneath the trees.
While the little one slept,
The dog listened.
He watched.
He waited.
And he stayed close.

Page 4: Morning
Morning warmed the leaves.
The dog lifted his nose.
And on the breeze came something familiar.
The scent of home.


Scene - Home
Page 1: Home
Then,
out from the trees,
there it was.
Home.

Page 2: Parents notice
Voices called out.
Footsteps hurried down the path.

Page 3: Reunion
Arms wrapped tight.
Happy tears fell.
The little one was safe.
The dog had brought him home.

Page 4: Safe again
That evening,
the house glowed warm.
The long day softened.
The woods felt far away.

Page 5: By the fireplace
A loyal nose.
A brave heart.
Home at last.
