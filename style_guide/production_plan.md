## Acceptance checklist
- Story action and emotion match the text.
- Dog/baby/parents match approved designs and relative scale.
- Location geometry, props, wardrobe, and time progression agree with adjacent frames.
- Creek stones and landmarks correspond across cameras.
- Background follows the selected simplified treatment; no widespread flecks or granular texture.
- Night foliage retains a bluish tint and restrained lighting; trunk/root moss stays broad and quiet.
- Ground leaves may use the selected candidate 3 treatment without importing its lighting or moss detail.
- Hands, paws, eyes, collar/tag, and contact with the ground are credible.
- Faces and action survive thumbnail/grayscale review; night remains readable.
- Actual game text and decorations remain legible.
- Export looks correct in-game and on representative displays.
