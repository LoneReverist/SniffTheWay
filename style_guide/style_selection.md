# Style selection — 2026-09-12

Decision: proceed with the simplified painterly style across all story backgrounds.

## Reference hierarchy

| Reference | Role | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| [Lost 1 — simplified](candidates/lost_v1/01_simplified_final.png) | Daytime painting treatment for the selected style | Still subject to final scene and display validation |
| [Night 1 — simplified](candidates/night_v1/01_simplified_final.png) | Primary night style and lighting reference; user's preferred night candidate | Preserve bluish foliage, broad shapes, and restrained lighting |
| [Night 3 — selective](candidates/night_v1/03_selective_final.png) | Ground leaves beneath the characters only | Reject its overly bright lighting and overly detailed moss at trunk/root edges |
| Dog and baby character sheets | Identity, anatomy, clothing | Adapt surface detail to simplified painting |

The matching background-only PNGs in each candidate folder remain available as environment references. Existing story artwork supplies narrative staging and location evidence, not the target texture density.

## User review

The moderate lost scene looked great, but simplified was judged a good fit for the game. In the night test, moderate looked worst. Between simplified and selective, simplified was preferred: its bluish leaf tint better conveys nighttime. Selective was too brightly lit and its trunk-edge moss too detailed, but the ground leaves underneath the characters were liked.

## Apply to future generation

Use broad dimensional painted foliage masses, selective edges, quiet moss patches, and restrained surface marks. At night retain cool blue/teal foliage and modest warm accents around the characters. Use grouped painted ground leaves informed by night 3 without importing its overall lighting or moss treatment. Keep the environment and characters at a compatible level of simplification.

Do not average all three candidates or use the moderate version as the default. When supplying night 3 as a reference, explicitly restrict its role to ground leaves. The primary reference takes precedence everywhere else.

This approves the style direction, not replacement of the game assets. No new images were generated for this update; continuity references, final scene refinements, export checks, and device/in-game validation remain to be completed.
